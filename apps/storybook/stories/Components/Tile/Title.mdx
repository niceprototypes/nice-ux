# Tile — Title

<Typography color="light">Renders a primary heading at the top of the tile. Additional Typography props can be forwarded via <code>titleProps</code>.</Typography>

<DocsRow>
  <Tile title="Title" />
  <Source language="tsx" code={`<Tile title="Title" />`} />
</DocsRow>

## titleProps

Forwarded to the title's `Typography`. Any prop from `TypographyProps` (except `children`) can be passed.

### titleProps.as

<DocsRow>
  <Tile title="Title" titleProps={{ as: "h1" }} />
  <Source language="tsx" code={`<Tile\n  title="Title"\n  titleProps={{ as: "h1" }}\n/>`} />
</DocsRow>
