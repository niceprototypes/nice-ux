## titleProps

Forwarded to the title's `Typography`. Any prop from `TypographyProps` (except `children`) can be passed.

### titleProps.as

<DataTableRow
  fullWidth
  columns={DEMO_CODE_COLUMNS}
  values={[
          <Tile
      title="Title"
      description="Description"
      titleProps={{ as: "h1" }}
    />,
          `<Tile\n  title="Title"\n  description="Description"\n  titleProps={{ as: "h1" }}\n/>`,
        ]}
/>
