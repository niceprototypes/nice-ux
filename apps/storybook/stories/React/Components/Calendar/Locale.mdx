## ar-EG (RTL)

<Ink color="light">
  Arabic labels and Arabic-Indic digits; the week starts on Saturday. For a
  right-to-left grid and mirrored arrow keys, the calendar also needs an RTL
  context: a <code>dir="rtl"</code> ancestor for layout, and a{" "}
  <code>StylesProvider</code> whose <code>locale</code> (or <code>dir</code>)
  resolves <code>useLocale().direction</code> to <code>rtl</code>. In this demo
  ArrowLeft moves to the next day.
</Ink>

  ]}
