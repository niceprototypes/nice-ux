Scroll: the scroll container every scrolling region renders through (`axis`, `showScrollbar`, `as`, `ref`). Scrollbars are hidden by default; the `scroll.scrollbarWidth` token sets the project default. Popover, List, Contents, Slider and the App shell's nav, sidebar, footer and scene region scroll through it.
